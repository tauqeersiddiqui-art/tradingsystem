Training workflow is two-step: run `python ml/dataset_builder.py` to generate `ml/models/training_dataset.csv` from `data/historical/nifty_1m_full.csv`, then `python ml/trainer.py` to train and gate-deploy champion models. Day classifier has its own CLI: `python -m ml.day_classifier --build --train`. Feedback retraining uses `python ml/feedback_trainer.py --retrain` or `--regime-models`. Models are backed up to `ml/models/backup_<timestamp>/` before any overwrite.
