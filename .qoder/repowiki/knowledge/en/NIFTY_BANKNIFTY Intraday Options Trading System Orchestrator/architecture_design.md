The root module is the process boundary: `login.py` performs a one-time Selenium+Edge OAuth flow to refresh the Zerodha `KITE_ACCESS_TOKEN` into `.env`, and `master_runner.py` is the long-lived supervisor that wires every child subsystem together at startup via `build_context()` (broker → `ExecutionEngine` → `TradingContext` → `LiveEngine` → `CapitalAllocator` → `IntradayMLLearner`) and `init_candle_builder()` (feeds historical NIFTY 1m CSV into `CandleBuilder`). The main `engine_loop()` drives the intraday cycle, while an in-process `EngineWatchdog` thread monitors the engine thread and auto-restarts it up to `WATCHDOG_MAX_RESTARTS` times, reconciling open positions from `data/runtime_state.json` on each restart. Cross-child contracts are all routed through `TradingContext`: the ML child (`ml.ml_intraday_learner`) supplies probabilities consumed by the engine's signal path, the data_store child persists state/journals read/written by both master and engine, the scripts child invokes this same runner for automation, and the research/backtest children reuse the identical signal/risk logic under test harnesses. Operational state (paper/dry-run modes, retrain locks, Telegram throttling) is coordinated centrally here rather than delegated to children.
